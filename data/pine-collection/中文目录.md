### 007. Broadening Wedge Scanner Pro [WavesUnchained]（指标）

[查看本地源码](indicators/market_structure/broadening_wedge_scanner_pro/broadening_wedge_scanner_pro.pine) · [查看上游源码](https://github.com/casoon/pine-scripts/blob/c7368182c246853f246911831171bc8258bc356a/indicators/market_structure/broadening_wedge_scanner_pro/broadening_wedge_scanner_pro.pine)

**中文备注：** 在搜索范围内滑动固定大小的已确认波段窗口，通过窗口外侧高点拟合上边界、外侧低点拟合下边界，寻找扩张楔形：下降扩张形（两线下降、下线更快，偏多）、上升扩张形（两线上升、上线更快，偏空）和对称扩张形（上线升、下线降，方向中性的喇叭形）。每个不同且互不重叠的有效楔形独立跟踪，直到确认突破而结束。

**原文摘录：** Slides a fixed-size window of confirmed swings across the search depth and fits two boundary lines (upper from the window's outer highs, lower from its outer lows) to find EXPANDING wedges — descending broadening (both lines fall, lower faster — bullish bias), ascending broadening (both lines rise, upper faster — bearish bias), or symmetric broadening (upper rising, lower falling — direction-neutral "megaphone"). Every distinct, non-overlapping valid wedge is tracked independently until a confirmed breakout resolves it.
